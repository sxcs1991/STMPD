Questions

Does STMPD work with fillable PDFs?

Yes. Fillable fields are listed in the panel and filled directly. You can still add extra fields and a signature on top.

What does “flattened” mean?

Each page is converted into an image inside the PDF, so entries and signatures can’t be edited afterwards. The trade-off is that the text in the finished file isn’t selectable.

Are my documents sent to an AI?

Only if you leave AI detection on. Then page images are sent for analysis. Turn it off to place every field yourself, entirely in your browser.

Why do I need to sign up for the free document?

One free document per person is enforced with a verified email, which is more reliable than tracking your device.

Is an e-signature legally valid?

STMPD records your agreement to sign electronically under E-SIGN and UETA principles. Whether a particular institution accepts an e-signed form is up to them, so check their requirements.
